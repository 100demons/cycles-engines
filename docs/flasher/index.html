Cycles Engines

Flash any combination of engines on the Elektron Model:Cycles, OS 1.13.

Nothing from Elektron is ever committed or uploaded — you bring your own official OS file.

Source and credits.

1

Pick your engines

Tick any combination. Each engine is a package: nothing in an engine knows its slot, so any two can live together. Engines are added after the six stock machines, in the order you tick them.

2

Load your official OS file

Get it from Elektron's support page (Model:Cycles, OS 1.13). It stays on your computer.

3

Connect your Model:Cycles over USB

4

Flash

Download the .syx … and send it with Elektron Transfer, or over MIDI with a DAW.
