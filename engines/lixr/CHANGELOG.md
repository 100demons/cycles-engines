# Changelog

## 3.0.0 — 2026-10-22

- First public version: the LIX2 design, with a sample-and-hold decimator in place of the filter.
- One tableless oscillator (sine, triangle, saw, rectangle), noise and clap layer, feedback FM, pitch envelope, soft saturator; four knobs (COLOR, SHAPE, SWEEP, RATE) plus the stock PITCH, DECAY, GATE, PUNCH and FINE.
- Tested in the emulator on the OS's own voice loop, and on a Model:Cycles from the model27 web flasher, alone and next to SAMPLE.
