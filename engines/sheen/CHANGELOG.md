# CHANGELOG — SHEEN

## 0.1 — 2026-10-27

- First package: a hybrid-rate hi-hat / metal voice for the Model:Cycles OS 1.13.
- Six 808 metal squares (SUM/RING), noise, a 48 kHz tick and air layer, a 24 kHz TPT voice filter with split
  body/sizzle envelopes, a two-pole chik resonator, a 128-sample shimmer comb, and the Heat chain.
- COLOR/SHAPE/SWEEP/CONTOUR macros; PUNCH opens, GATE chokes at the note end, velocity darkens and shortens.
- Home pair: closed hat with PUNCH off, open hat with PUNCH on; the closed step chokes the open tail.
- Host checks (§9.1–9.3): all pass except the roll's step-ratio check (reported in PROGRESS.md).
- Device: sample-exact against the host for 22 scripts; six-voice CPU is 2.58× TONE (over budget, reported).
- Demos: 00-groove plus all 17 recipes and macro points in `build/sheen-demos/` (gitignored).
